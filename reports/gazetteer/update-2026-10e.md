# Gazetteer pass: update 2026-10e (untitled names; Bar/Ben X toponymic)

**Untitled names.** The 716 multi-word Talmud-names entries with no rabbinic title (no Rabbi/Rav/Rabban/
Mar/Abba; no anonymous 'X’s wife' entries, since Figures on the Fringes lists only named people). A
script aligned each entry to its Aramaic phrase, then:
- dropped people the Bavli also calls רב/רבי/רבן/מר + the same name;
- checked the rest in the paper, including Figures on the Fringes, with a loose spelling match.

165 were left; I reviewed them by hand. Most are already in the paper (the Fringes lists, the
honorific lists) or were misalignments.

**Bar/Ben X toponymic.** The 'bar/ben X' parts of all entries were crossed with the toponym, biblical-
place and nations gazetteers. The real toponymic ones (בן גמלא, בר היני, בן ציון) are already in the
paper; בר אסיא and בר גזא are uncertain.

14 rows in `update-2026-10e.csv`: **7 new**, 3 uncertain, 2 variant-of-existing, 2 already-in-paper.
