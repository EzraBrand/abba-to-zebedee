# Gazetteer pass: family-occupation-traits

Sections: Etiological; Patronymic & familial (all subsections); Occupational; Personal status / personality / physical trait; Double names; Unclear etymology; Pseudonyms; Slurs. Source: `talmud_names_gazetteer.txt`. 94 rows in `family-occupation-traits.csv`.

**Counts:** new 18, uncertain 19, variant-of-existing 8, already-in-paper 49.

**Strongest new items**
- Etiological: **יוסף שריא** (AZ 37a:3, the Talmud explains the name and objects "יוסף אסרא מיבעי ליה"); **בן חמצן** (Kid 53a:9, "they called him ben Ḥamtzan until the day he died"); **בן סטדא / בן פנדירא** (San 67a:15, "סטת דא מבעלה"). These two also fit Slurs, and בן סטדא also fits Matronymic.
- Female kinship forms are not in the paper: **דביתהו דX** (wife of; e.g. ברוריא דביתהו דרבי מאיר, אימא שלום דביתהו דרבי אליעזר), **אימיה דX / אמו של X** (female teknonymy, e.g. אימיה דרמי בר חמא, איפרא הורמיז אימיה דשבור מלכא), **ברתיה דX / בת X** (female patronymic), **אחתיה דX** (sister of).
- Other kin: **בר חמוה דX / בן חמיו של X** (brother-in-law), **אחי אמו של X / אחות דאמיה** (maternal uncle).
- Matronymic: **בנה של מרתא בת בייתוס** (Suk 52b:15).
- Occupational: **צבעא** (dyer), **תנא** (אשיאן תנא דבי רבי אמי), **תוראה** (ox herder), **חקלאה** (farmer; Rav Anan's mocking name for Rav Naḥman), **המלבש** (Pineḥas, keeper of the vestments), **כנדוקא** (jug maker, probably Greek).
- Traits and status: **הסבר** (R. Yehuda "the Keen"), **אמתא** (בני יודן אמתא).

**Notes**
- The "uncertain" rows are mostly non-sages known by patronymic alone (בר קמצא, בן קמצר, בן קטין, בן דרוסאי, בר קיפוק, ...). fn 203 limits that list to sages and points to a "Commoners" section, but v18.2 does not have that section.
- Sections the paper does not have yet: (1) female naming by kin (wife, mother, daughter, sister of X); this could be a subsection under Patronymic and Other Familial Relations. (2) In-law and maternal-line relations beyond חמוה / חתניה.
- Two readings differ from the paper: Steinsaltz reads תנורא ("oven maker", now under Unclear Etymology) and סבולאה ("porter", now under Toponymic X-ah) as occupational.
- No new items for "Double Names". יתמא (orphan) is cited only in fn 73. It could be listed under Personal status.
