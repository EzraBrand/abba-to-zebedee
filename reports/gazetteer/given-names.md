# given-names: gazetteer vs. given-name sections (v18.2)

Rows: 70 — new 18, uncertain 24, variant-of-existing 8, already-in-paper 20. Details in `given-names.csv`.
Method: 3,779 gazetteer entries reduced to about 800 given names and 270 father-only names. Each was aligned to Bavli Hebrew and checked against the whole paper.

## Strongest new items
- Greek/Latin: קלונימוס / קלוניקוס (Onkelos's father; Greek Kalonymos), פפייס (R. Pappeyas; Pappias), אבקולס (Zekharya b. Avkolas; Eukolos).
- Persian: סורחב (Surḥav bar Pappa; Sohrab). He is a Jew with an Iranian name. The Persian section now has only the gentile names from Gittin 11a.
- Hebrew list gaps: חננאל, מלכיו / מלכיא, שפטיה, אליקים (only in English in the Bareket quote), עובדיה, דניאל (Rav Daniel), אבידן, אלישמע, ששון.
- Aramaic list gaps (rare): עיליש, נהילאי, שישא, ביסא, שבא.

## Gaps for existing items (already in paper, but not in the relevant list)
- Greek/Latin: רומנוס (only in No surname, though Kaganoff calls Romanus Latin), ורדימוס (only fn574), ננס (only under Physical trait).
- Persian: איזגדר (Yazdegerd) and פירוז (Peroz) are only in footnotes.
- Etymology lists do not have very common names: חסדא, חנינא, חיננא, ששת (Aramaic); יאשיה, מתנה, פדת, עזריה, מאיר, שמאי (Hebrew).
- Variants to add: יוסטאי, זונן, אבודימי, דוסאי, עקביא, אחאי, זירא.
- Aramaic list items 28 and 29 are the same item twice (זעירא / זעירי).

## Mononyms (`mononym_counts.txt`)
- That file counts 1,733 standalone "Rabbi" and 978 standalone "Rabba". The list has רב and רבא, but not רבי or רבה.
- Other people with no title: שמעיה, אבטליון, מנחם (Ḥagigah 16a), and לוי (Levi bar Sisi, `uncertain`).

## Patterns the paper does not yet have
- Iranian names of Jews: סורחב, מונבז, maybe דרו and מהבאי. The current Persian section has only gentile names.
- Gentile Greek/Latin names in the Bavli: אנטונינוס, אסוירוס, נירון, פרוקלוס. The lists are of Jewish names, so these are all `uncertain`.
