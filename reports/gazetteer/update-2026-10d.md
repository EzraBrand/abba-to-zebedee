# Gazetteer pass: update 2026-10d (one-word names; placed by who they are)

Input: the 203 one-word entries of the Talmud-names gazetteer, without groups (Romans, Sadducees …),
honorifics and the angels already in the paper. Each name was aligned to its Aramaic form, and the
Bavli was searched for it with a title (רב/רבי/מר …) or a patronymic. Each candidate was checked in the
paper, which since 15-Oct includes *Figures on the Fringes* (Jewish commoners, women, non-Jews, angels
and demons) and the Ben Bag Bag list.

Placement: sages known by one name go to Mononyms. Following the author's instruction, women and
non-Jews go only to the Figures on the Fringes lists, and so do Jewish non-rabbis.
