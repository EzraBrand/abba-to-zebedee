# Gazetteer pass: update 2026-10 (entries added since the 10-Oct passes)

Input: the gazetteer entries added between the 20-Sep and 15-Oct versions of
[talmud-nlp-indexer/data](https://github.com/EzraBrand/talmud-nlp-indexer/tree/main/data). That is
+98 Talmud names, +7 toponyms, +3 nations and +23 biblical names. The four Ish X places that the
Alma map alignment linked to Bavli forms were also checked. Each Hebrew form was found in the Bavli
(Steinsaltz English → parallel Aramaic) and searched in `paper/paper.md` and all list CSVs, without nikud.

50 rows in `update-2026-10.csv`: **7 new**, 4 uncertain, 12 variant-of-existing, 27 already-in-paper.
Most added entries are already in the paper, or they fit a format it lists (זקני X, דייני X, בני X, ההוא X, דבי X, אבוה דX).

New items:
- Word Play on Name: איכו (Taanit 25a:7, 'איכו נימטו כשוריך').
- Pattern katal: פישון הגמל (Yevamot 107b:8). The baraita also puns on the name ('במדה כפושה מדד').
- Occupational Other: בעיא מיכסא (Sanhedrin 44b:13). Check the reading before accepting.
- Other Familial Relationships: כלתיה דX / כלתו של X (11 segments). The list has חמוה דX and חתניה דX but no daughter-in-law.
- Individuals: עבדיה / אמתיה דX, a slave or maidservant named by the master (13 segments).
- Groups: רבותינו שבX (Ketubot 50a:12 שביבנה, Eruvin 65b:18 שבדרום).
- Rabbi; Rav: the suffix רבינו (משה רבינו, 77 segments; רבינו הקדוש, 6).

Uncertain: given names that the paper lacks but that need an etymology to place (בירא, שיבחת,
תוטאי), and זכריה בן קבוטל: there is no evidence that קבוטל is the mother.

Correction (15-Oct): X הרשע was first listed as new; the Personality list already has הרשע / רשיעא.

Corrections: the paper lists בר המדורי, and the Vilna text reads בר המדודי (Yoma 87b:7).
